Online Retail Business Performance Dashboard

Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Online Retail Business Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyses historical online retail transactions to provide insights into revenue performance, product demand, order distribution across countries, and sales trends over time.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into visual insights that can support business monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

Dataset

The project uses the UCI Online Retail Dataset.

The dataset contains transactional records from a UK-based online retailer between December 2010 and December 2011.

Key dataset fields:

InvoiceNo – Transaction or invoice number.

StockCode – Unique product code.

Description – Product description.

Quantity – Number of units purchased.

InvoiceDate – Date and time of the transaction.

UnitPrice – Price per unit.

CustomerID – Customer identification number.

Country – Customer's country.

Dashboard Features

The dashboard contains two interactive slicers and four visualisations.

Interactive Slicers

Year: Filters the dashboard according to the selected year.

Country: Filters the dashboard based on the selected country.

Visualisations

Visualisation

Description

Line Chart – Revenue Trends per Month

Displays monthly revenue performance and highlights changes in revenue over time.

Bar Chart – Top 10 Products

Identifies the ten leading products based on quantity sold.

Donut Chart – Orders by Top 5 Countries

Illustrates how orders are distributed among the five leading countries.

Area Chart – Quantity Sold over the Months

Shows monthly changes in the quantity of products sold.

Tools and Technologies

Microsoft Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development and data visualisation.

Power Query – Data preparation and transformation.

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Creation of analytical measures.

UCI Machine Learning Repository – Dataset source.

Business Questions Addressed

The dashboard helps explore the following questions:

How does revenue change from month to month?

Which ten products have the highest sales quantities?

Which five countries account for the largest share of orders?

How does product demand change over time?

How do revenue and product demand vary across different years and countries?

Project Objectives

Analyse historical online retail transaction data.

Identify monthly revenue and sales quantity trends.

Determine the products with the highest demand.

Examine the geographical distribution of orders.

Provide an interactive dashboard for exploring business performance.
